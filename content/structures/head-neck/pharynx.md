name: Pharynx
type: organ
region: head-neck
subregion: Pharynx & larynx
taName: Pharynx
aka: [Throat, Nasopharynx, Oropharynx, Laryngopharynx]
summary: Fibromuscular tube from the skull base to C6, shared by the airway and the food passage. It is divided into nasopharynx, oropharynx and laryngopharynx, and the site of Zenker diverticulum and nasopharyngeal carcinoma.
tags: [pharynx, swallowing, ent]
highYield: false
status: draft
anatomy:
  location: Behind the nasal cavity, mouth and larynx, in front of the cervical vertebrae, from the skull base to the lower border of the cricoid cartilage (**C6**), where it continues as the [[esophagus]].
  parts:
    - "**Nasopharynx**: behind the choanae; contains the **pharyngeal tonsil (adenoids)** and the openings of the **pharyngotympanic tubes** (see [[middle-ear]])"
    - "**Oropharynx**: behind the mouth, from the soft palate to the tip of the epiglottis; contains the [[palatine-tonsils]] and the base of the [[tongue]]"
    - "**Laryngopharynx (hypopharynx)**: behind the [[larynx]]; the **piriform recesses** lie on each side of the laryngeal inlet (where fish bones lodge)"
    - "**Muscles**: superior, middle and inferior constrictors (the inferior has thyropharyngeus and **cricopharyngeus** parts); stylopharyngeus, palatopharyngeus, salpingopharyngeus"
    - "**Killian triangle (dehiscence)**: weak area between thyropharyngeus and cricopharyngeus"
  bloodSupply: Ascending pharyngeal, facial, lingual and maxillary arteries (branches of the [[external-carotid-artery]]) and the inferior thyroid artery.
  venousDrainage: Pharyngeal venous plexus → internal jugular vein.
  lymphatics: Retropharyngeal and deep cervical nodes (see [[cervical-lymph-nodes]]).
  innervation: >-
    **Pharyngeal plexus**: motor from **[[vagus-nerve|CN X]]** (all constrictors and most muscles) except **stylopharyngeus ([[glossopharyngeal-nerve|CN IX]])**;
    sensory from **CN IX** (oropharynx — afferent limb of the **gag reflex**; the efferent limb is CN X) and CN X
    (laryngopharynx); nasopharynx from V2.
  relations: Behind lies the retropharyngeal space (see [[deep-neck-spaces]]); laterally the parapharyngeal space and the carotid sheath.
clinical:
  - title: Zenker (pharyngoesophageal) diverticulum
    highYield: true
    mechanism: Pulsion diverticulum through **Killian triangle** above the cricopharyngeus, from cricopharyngeal dysfunction; older adults.
    presentation: '**Dysphagia, regurgitation of undigested food, halitosis**, gurgling in the neck, aspiration. Diagnose with a **barium swallow**; endoscopy carries a perforation risk.'
  - title: Nasopharyngeal carcinoma
    highYield: false
    mechanism: Associated with **EBV**, higher incidence in Southern China and North Africa.
    presentation: Unilateral **serous otitis media in an adult** (eustachian tube obstruction), epistaxis, nasal obstruction, neck nodes, cranial nerve palsies.
  - title: Adenoid hypertrophy
    highYield: false
    mechanism: Enlarged pharyngeal tonsil in children.
    presentation: Mouth breathing, snoring and obstructive sleep apnea, hyponasal speech, recurrent otitis media with effusion.
  - title: Oropharyngeal cancer
    highYield: true
    mechanism: Increasingly **HPV-16-related** squamous cell carcinoma (tonsil, base of tongue), often in younger nonsmokers; otherwise tobacco and alcohol.
    presentation: Persistent sore throat, dysphagia, **referred otalgia**, or a painless neck mass (cystic nodal metastasis).
pance:
  - "Elderly with regurgitation of undigested food + halitosis → **Zenker diverticulum** → barium swallow."
  - "Adult with new unilateral serous otitis media → rule out **nasopharyngeal carcinoma** (EBV)."
  - "Gag reflex: afferent **CN IX**, efferent **CN X**."
  - "Adult with a new neck mass → think **head and neck cancer** (HPV oropharyngeal)."
imaging:
  xray: '**Barium swallow (videofluoroscopy)** for Zenker diverticulum and dysphagia; lateral neck film for adenoids and retropharyngeal swelling.'
  ct: Contrast CT neck for tumors and deep neck infections.
  mri: Nasopharyngeal carcinoma extent and skull base involvement.
  keyViews:
    - "Barium swallow / modified barium swallow"
exam:
  landmarks: The pharynx ends at the cricoid cartilage (C6).
  testing: >-
    Inspect the oropharynx with a tongue depressor; **nasopharyngoscopy/laryngoscopy** (flexible endoscope) for the
    nasopharynx and laryngopharynx. **CN IX/X**: palate elevation (uvula deviates **away** from a CN X lesion), gag
    reflex, swallowing, voice.
quiz:
  - stem: >-
      A 74-year-old man has progressive dysphagia, regurgitation of food eaten days earlier, bad breath and a gurgling
      sound in his neck when he swallows. What is the most likely diagnosis?
    choices:
      - Achalasia
      - Zenker diverticulum
      - Esophageal carcinoma
      - Plummer–Vinson syndrome
      - Globus sensation
    answer: B
    explanation: >-
      **Zenker diverticulum** — a pulsion pouch through Killian triangle above the cricopharyngeus — causes regurgitation
      of undigested food, halitosis and a gurgling neck mass in older adults. Diagnose with a barium swallow.
  - stem: Which muscle of the pharynx is supplied by the glossopharyngeal nerve?
    choices:
      - Superior constrictor
      - Palatopharyngeus
      - Stylopharyngeus
      - Cricopharyngeus
      - Salpingopharyngeus
    answer: C
    explanation: >-
      **Stylopharyngeus** is the only muscle supplied by **CN IX**; all the other pharyngeal muscles are supplied by the
      vagus via the pharyngeal plexus.
