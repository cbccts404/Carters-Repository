---
name: Intercostal space
type: space
region: thorax
subregion: Thoracic wall
taName: Spatium intercostale
summary: Space between adjacent ribs containing three muscle layers and the intercostal neurovascular bundle, which runs along the lower border of the rib above.
tags: [thoracic-wall, procedures]
systems: [pulmonary, musculoskeletal]
highYield: true
status: draft
anatomy:
  location: Between adjacent ribs, eleven spaces on each side.
  boundaries:
    superior: Inferior border of the rib above (with its costal groove)
    inferior: Superior border of the rib below
    superficial: Skin, subcutaneous tissue, and overlying muscles (pectoral, serratus anterior, back muscles)
    deep: Endothoracic fascia and parietal pleura
  contents:
    - "**External intercostal muscle**: fibers run inferoanteriorly (like hands in front pockets); replaced anteriorly by the external intercostal membrane"
    - "**Internal intercostal muscle**: fibers run at right angles to the externals; replaced posteriorly by the internal intercostal membrane"
    - "**Innermost intercostal muscle** (with subcostales and transversus thoracis in the same layer)"
    - "**Intercostal neurovascular bundle**: from superior to inferior, **V**ein, **A**rtery, **N**erve (VAN), in the costal groove between the internal and innermost layers"
    - "Collateral branches of the vessels and nerve along the upper border of the rib below"
  communications: Posteriorly with the paravertebral space. The intercostal nerves continue anteriorly into the abdominal wall (T7–T11).
  notes: >-
    **Intercostal nerves** are the ventral rami of T1–T11 (T12 is the subcostal nerve). They supply the intercostal
    muscles and a band of skin (dermatome); **T4 ≈ nipple, T10 ≈ umbilicus**. **Posterior intercostal arteries** come
    from the thoracic aorta (spaces 3–11) and the supreme intercostal artery (spaces 1–2). **Anterior intercostal
    arteries** come from the [[internal-thoracic-artery]] (spaces 1–6) and the musculophrenic artery. Posterior
    intercostal veins drain to the [[azygos-vein]] system.
clinical:
  - title: Needle and tube placement (thoracentesis, chest tube)
    highYield: true
    mechanism: The main neurovascular bundle lies under the lower border of each rib.
    presentation: >-
      Insert needles and tubes **just above the upper border of the rib** (the lower rib of the space) to avoid the
      intercostal vein, artery and nerve. Posteriorly, near the angle, the bundle is less protected by the costal groove,
      so avoid going too medial. Intercostal artery injury can cause significant hemothorax.
  - title: Herpes zoster (shingles)
    highYield: true
    mechanism: Reactivation of varicella-zoster virus in a dorsal root ganglion, spreading along the intercostal nerve.
    presentation: >-
      Burning pain followed by a vesicular rash in a **dermatomal band** that doesn't cross the midline. Thoracic
      dermatomes are the most commonly affected.
  - title: Intercostal nerve block
    highYield: false
    mechanism: Local anesthetic injected near the intercostal nerve at the inferior border of the rib, often at the angle.
    presentation: Analgesia for rib fractures or thoracic surgery. Blocking adjacent spaces improves coverage because of overlap.
pance:
  - "Neurovascular bundle order, top to bottom: **VAN** (vein, artery, nerve), under the rib above."
  - "Thoracentesis / chest tube: go **over the top of the lower rib**."
  - "Dermatomes: **T4 nipple**, **T10 umbilicus**."
  - "Herpes zoster: unilateral dermatomal vesicles; the thoracic region is most common."
imaging:
  xray: Intercostal spaces are counted on the chest X-ray to describe lesion location and line or tube positions.
  ct: Shows the intercostal muscles, vessels (with contrast) and pleura. Useful for chest wall masses and hemothorax from intercostal artery bleeding.
  ultrasound: >-
    Essential for **pleural procedures**: identify the ribs (acoustic shadows), the pleural line, and fluid; confirm the
    diaphragm position; color Doppler can show the intercostal artery before needle insertion.
  keyViews:
    - "Ultrasound in the longitudinal plane across two ribs (\"bat sign\": ribs as wings, pleural line as body)"
exam:
  landmarks: Identify the 2nd intercostal space just below the sternal angle and count down. Posteriorly, the inferior angle of the scapula marks about the 7th rib or intercostal space.
  palpation: Palpate the spaces for tenderness, bulging (effusion, rarely) or retraction.
  testing: Map sensory deficits or zoster rashes to dermatomes. Observe intercostal retractions (increased work of breathing).

      risk?
    choices:

      - Intercostal neurovascular bundle
      - Phrenic nerve
      - Thoracic duct
      - Long thoracic nerve
    answer: B
    explanation: >-
      The **intercostal vein, artery and nerve** run in the costal groove along the **inferior border** of each rib.
      Needles should pass just above the superior border of the rib below.

      - Artery, vein, nerve
      - Vein, artery, nerve
      - Vein, nerve, artery
      - Artery, nerve, vein
    answer: C
    explanation: "From superior to inferior within the costal groove: **V**ein, **A**rtery, **N**erve (**VAN**)."
  - stem: A 72-year-old man has burning pain followed by a vesicular band-like rash at the level of his umbilicus on one side. Which dermatome is involved?
    choices:
      - T4
      - T6
      - T10
      - L1
      - L4
    answer: C
    explanation: >-
      The **umbilicus** corresponds to the **T10** dermatome. T4 is at the nipple. Herpes zoster follows a single
      dermatome and doesn't cross the midline.
  - stem: The anterior intercostal arteries of spaces 1–6 arise from which artery?
    choices:
      - Internal thoracic artery
      - Thoracic aorta
      - Supreme intercostal artery
      - Lateral thoracic artery
      - Musculophrenic artery
    answer: A
    explanation: >-
      The **internal thoracic artery** gives the anterior intercostal arteries of spaces 1–6; the musculophrenic artery
      supplies the lower spaces. The **posterior** intercostal arteries come from the thoracic aorta (spaces 3–11) and
      the supreme intercostal artery (spaces 1–2).
  - stem: Which intercostal muscle has fibers running inferoanteriorly, like hands in front pockets?
    choices:
      - Internal intercostal
      - External intercostal
      - Innermost intercostal
      - Transversus thoracis
      - Subcostalis
    answer: B
    explanation: >-
      **External intercostal** fibers run **inferoanteriorly** ("hands in front pockets"). The internal intercostals run
      at right angles to them. The neurovascular bundle lies between the internal and innermost layers.
