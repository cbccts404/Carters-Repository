type: organ

subregion: Cerebrum
taName: Systema limbicum
aka: [Hippocampus, Amygdala, Cingulate gyrus, Fornix, Papez circuit, Wernicke–Korsakoff syndrome]
summary: Network of medial temporal and diencephalic structures for memory, emotion and behavior. The hippocampus and mammillary bodies are damaged in Alzheimer disease, Wernicke–Korsakoff syndrome and HSV encephalitis.
tags: [cerebrum, memory, emotion]
highYield: true
status: draft
anatomy:
  location: Medial rim of the cerebral hemisphere around the diencephalon, mainly the **medial temporal lobe**.
  parts:
    - "**Hippocampus** (medial temporal lobe, floor of the temporal horn) — forms new declarative memories; CA1 neurons are very sensitive to **hypoxia**"
    - "**Amygdala** (anterior to the hippocampus) — fear, emotional memory, autonomic responses"
    - "**Fornix** — output tract of the hippocampus to the **mammillary bodies**"
    - "**Mammillary bodies** ([[hypothalamus]]) → **mammillothalamic tract** → **anterior nucleus of the [[thalamus]]** → **cingulate gyrus** → parahippocampal/entorhinal cortex → hippocampus (**Papez circuit**)"
    - "**Cingulate gyrus**, parahippocampal gyrus, septal nuclei, nucleus accumbens (reward), olfactory connections"
  bloodSupply: Posterior cerebral artery (hippocampus), anterior choroidal artery (amygdala, hippocampus), ACA (cingulate) — see [[cerebral-arteries]].
  venousDrainage: Basal vein → great cerebral vein.
  notes: Receives olfactory input directly ([[olfactory-nerve]]); outputs to the hypothalamus and prefrontal cortex.
  relations: The hippocampus lies in the floor of the **temporal horn of the lateral ventricle** (see [[ventricular-system]]).
clinical:
  - title: Wernicke encephalopathy and Korsakoff syndrome
    highYield: true
    mechanism: '**Thiamine (B1) deficiency** — alcohol use disorder, malnutrition, hyperemesis, bariatric surgery; damages the **mammillary bodies** and medial thalamus.'
    presentation: '**Wernicke**: **confusion, ophthalmoplegia/nystagmus, ataxia** (not all three are always present) — give **IV thiamine before glucose**. **Korsakoff**: permanent **anterograde and retrograde amnesia with confabulation**.'
  - title: Alzheimer disease
    highYield: true
    mechanism: Amyloid plaques and neurofibrillary tangles; early loss in the **hippocampus and entorhinal cortex**, cholinergic deficit (nucleus basalis of Meynert).
    presentation: Insidious **short-term memory loss** first, then language, visuospatial and executive decline; MRI shows **hippocampal/medial temporal atrophy**. Cholinesterase inhibitors (donepezil), memantine.
  - title: HSV encephalitis and temporal lobe epilepsy
    highYield: true
    mechanism: '**HSV-1** has a predilection for the **medial temporal and orbitofrontal lobes**; **mesial temporal sclerosis** (hippocampal) is the most common cause of focal epilepsy in adults.'
    presentation: '**HSV encephalitis**: fever, headache, confusion, behavior change, seizures, aphasia; MRI temporal lobe changes, CSF HSV PCR — start **IV acyclovir immediately**. **Temporal lobe seizures**: aura (déjà vu, rising epigastric sensation, smells), staring, lip-smacking automatisms.'
  - title: Klüver–Bucy syndrome
    highYield: false
    mechanism: Bilateral amygdala (anterior temporal) damage, e.g. HSV encephalitis, trauma.
    presentation: Hyperorality, hypersexuality, placidity (loss of fear) and visual agnosia.
pance:
  - "Confusion + ophthalmoplegia + ataxia in an alcoholic → **Wernicke** → **thiamine before glucose**."
  - "Amnesia + confabulation → **Korsakoff** (mammillary bodies)."
  - "Fever + confusion + temporal lobe changes → **HSV encephalitis** → IV acyclovir now."
  - "Déjà vu aura + automatisms → **temporal lobe (hippocampal) seizure**."
  - "Early Alzheimer → **hippocampal atrophy**, short-term memory loss."
imaging:
  mri: '**MRI** — hippocampal atrophy (Alzheimer), mesial temporal sclerosis (epilepsy protocol), temporal lobe T2/FLAIR signal in HSV encephalitis, mammillary body changes in Wernicke encephalopathy.'
  ct: Often normal early in HSV encephalitis and Wernicke encephalopathy.
  keyViews:
    - "EEG — temporal spikes; periodic lateralized discharges in HSV encephalitis"
exam:
  testing: 'Orientation, **recall of three words at 5 minutes** (anterograde memory), remote memory, cognitive screening (MoCA/MMSE); eye movements and gait (Wernicke); mood and behavior.'
quiz:
  - stem: >-
      A 50-year-old man with alcohol use disorder is confused, has horizontal nystagmus with bilateral lateral rectus
      weakness, and a wide-based gait. What is the most appropriate immediate treatment?
    choices:
      - IV dextrose alone
      - IV thiamine, before glucose
      - Oral folate only
      - Lorazepam alone
      - Lumbar puncture then observe
    answer: B
    explanation: >-
      **Wernicke encephalopathy** (confusion, ophthalmoplegia, ataxia) from thiamine deficiency; give **IV thiamine
      immediately and before glucose**, which can precipitate or worsen it.
  - stem: >-
      A 34-year-old has 3 days of fever, headache and odd behavior, then a seizure. MRI shows right medial temporal lobe
      hyperintensity. What is the most appropriate treatment?
    choices:
      - IV ceftriaxone only
      - IV acyclovir
      - Oral valacyclovir as outpatient
      - Prednisone
      - Observation
    answer: B
    explanation: >-
      **HSV-1 encephalitis** favors the medial temporal lobes. Start **IV acyclovir** as soon as it is suspected; confirm
      with CSF HSV PCR.
