name: Inferior mesenteric artery
type: artery
region: abdomen
subregion: Posterior abdominal wall
taName: Arteria mesenterica inferior
aka: [IMA]
summary: Artery of the hindgut, arising from the aorta at L3. It supplies the distal transverse colon to the upper rectum, and its ligation during aortic surgery puts the watershed colon at risk.
tags: [vascular, hindgut]
highYield: true
status: draft
anatomy:
  location: Arises from the anterior aorta at about **L3**, behind the 3rd/4th part of the duodenum, 3–4 cm above the bifurcation.
  origin: "[[abdominal-aorta]]"
  course: Descends retroperitoneally to the left, crossing the left common iliac artery to become the superior rectal artery in the pelvis.
  branches:
    - "**Left colic artery** (descending colon, distal transverse colon)"
    - "**Sigmoid arteries** (sigmoid colon)"
    - "**Superior rectal artery** (upper rectum), its terminal branch"
  supplies: '**Hindgut**: distal third of the transverse colon, descending colon, sigmoid colon, upper rectum.'
  anastomoses: >-
    The **marginal artery (of Drummond)** along the colon connects it with the middle colic ([[superior-mesenteric-artery]]).
    The superior rectal artery anastomoses with the middle and inferior rectal arteries (internal iliac). Watershed zones:
    **splenic flexure** and **rectosigmoid**.
  relations: The inferior mesenteric vein runs to its left and ascends to join the splenic vein. A **horseshoe kidney** is typically trapped beneath the IMA during its ascent.
clinical:
  - title: Ischemic colitis after aortic surgery
    highYield: true
    mechanism: The IMA is often sacrificed (or covered by an endograft) during AAA repair. If collaterals are poor, the left colon becomes ischemic.
    presentation: Left-sided abdominal pain and bloody diarrhea after aortic repair (the sigmoid and splenic flexure are most affected).
  - title: Horseshoe kidney
    highYield: false
    mechanism: Fusion of the lower poles of the kidneys; ascent is halted when the isthmus catches under the IMA.
    presentation: Usually asymptomatic. Increased risk of obstruction, stones and infection. Associated with Turner syndrome.
pance:
  - "IMA (L3) = **hindgut**: distal 1/3 transverse colon → upper rectum."
  - "Branches: **left colic, sigmoid, superior rectal**."
  - "**Horseshoe kidney** is trapped under the IMA."
  - "Post-AAA repair bloody diarrhea → ischemic colitis (IMA territory)."
imaging:
  ct: CT angiography shows its origin, patency and the marginal artery; colonic wall thickening in ischemic colitis.
  keyViews:
    - "CT angiography"
    - "Colonoscopy/sigmoidoscopy to assess ischemic colitis after aortic surgery"
exam:
  testing: LLQ tenderness and bloody stool after aortic surgery should prompt evaluation for ischemic colitis.
