---
name: Inferior vena cava
type: vein
region: abdomen
subregion: Posterior abdominal wall
taName: Vena cava inferior
aka: [IVC]
summary: Largest vein of the body, returning blood from below the diaphragm to the right atrium. It lies to the right of the aorta, passes through the diaphragm at T8, and is compressed by the gravid uterus in the supine position.
tags: [vascular, posterior-abdominal-wall]
highYield: true
status: draft
anatomy:
  location: Retroperitoneal, to the **right** of the [[abdominal-aorta]] on the lumbar vertebral bodies, then in a groove on the posterior surface of the [[liver]].
  formation: Union of the **right and left common iliac veins** at about **L5**, behind the right common iliac artery.
  course: Ascends to the right of the aorta, passes through the **caval opening of the central tendon of the diaphragm at T8**, and enters the right atrium.
  tributaries:
    - Common iliac veins
    - Lumbar veins (3rd and 4th)
    - "**Right gonadal vein** (the left drains into the left renal vein)"
    - "**Renal veins**: the **left renal vein** is longer, crosses anterior to the aorta just below the SMA, and receives the left gonadal and left suprarenal veins"
    - "**Right suprarenal vein** (short, directly into the IVC)"
    - Inferior phrenic veins
    - "**Hepatic veins** (just below the diaphragm)"
  drainage: Right atrium of the heart.
  relations: >-
    Anterior: right common iliac artery, root of the mesentery, 3rd part of the [[duodenum]], head of the [[pancreas]],
    the portal vein and the omental foramen ([[lesser-sac]]), and the liver. Posterior: right sympathetic trunk, right
    renal artery, right crus of the diaphragm.
clinical:
  - title: Supine hypotensive syndrome of pregnancy (aortocaval compression)
    highYield: true
    mechanism: The gravid uterus compresses the IVC (and aorta) when the patient lies supine, typically after about 20 weeks, reducing venous return.
    presentation: Hypotension, dizziness, pallor or fetal distress when supine, relieved by **left lateral tilt**. Manual left uterine displacement during maternal resuscitation.
  - title: IVC filter
    highYield: true
    mechanism: A filter placed in the infrarenal IVC traps emboli from the legs.
    presentation: Indicated for acute proximal DVT or PE when **anticoagulation is contraindicated** (or has failed). Removable filters should be retrieved when no longer needed.
  - title: Tumor extension and obstruction
    highYield: false
    mechanism: '**Renal cell carcinoma** can grow into the renal vein and IVC as a tumor thrombus; IVC thrombosis or compression.'
    presentation: Bilateral leg edema, dilated abdominal wall collateral veins (flow upward), a right-sided varicocele, and Budd–Chiari physiology if the thrombus reaches the hepatic segment.
pance:
  - "IVC forms at **L5**, passes the diaphragm at **T8**."
  - "Right gonadal vein → IVC; **left gonadal vein → left renal vein**."
  - "Pregnant patient hypotensive when supine → **left lateral positioning** (aortocaval compression)."
  - "IVC filter when anticoagulation is **contraindicated** in DVT/PE."
imaging:
  ct: Contrast CT shows thrombus, tumor extension (RCC), congenital anomalies (duplicated or left-sided IVC, azygos continuation), and filter position.
  ultrasound: >-
    Visible in the subcostal view. **IVC diameter and inspiratory collapse** are used at the bedside as a rough guide to
    right atrial pressure and fluid status (limited reliability in ventilated patients). Doppler for thrombosis.
    {{verify: IVC-based volume assessment has significant limitations}}
  keyViews:
    - "Subcostal long-axis view of the IVC entering the right atrium"
    - "CT venography"
exam:
  landmarks: Lies to the right of the midline on the lumbar spine; not palpable.
  testing: >-
    Look for bilateral leg edema and abdominal wall collateral veins. In IVC obstruction, blood in the periumbilical
    collaterals flows **upward**; in portal hypertension (caput medusae), it flows away from the umbilicus.

    answer: B
    explanation: >-
      **Aortocaval compression** by the gravid uterus reduces venous return. **Left lateral tilt** (or manual left uterine

  - stem: The left gonadal vein drains into which vessel?
    choices:
      - Inferior vena cava
      - Left renal vein
      - Left common iliac vein
      - Inferior mesenteric vein
      - Hemiazygos vein
    answer: B
    explanation: The **left gonadal vein drains into the left renal vein**; the right gonadal vein drains directly into the IVC.
  - stem: A patient with a proximal DVT has an intracranial hemorrhage. What is the most appropriate way to prevent pulmonary embolism?
    choices:
      - Therapeutic heparin
      - Warfarin
      - Inferior vena cava filter
      - Aspirin
      - Thrombolysis
    answer: C
    explanation: When anticoagulation is **contraindicated** (active bleeding), an **IVC filter** can prevent PE from a leg DVT.
