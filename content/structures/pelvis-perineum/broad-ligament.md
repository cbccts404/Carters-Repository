name: Broad ligament
type: ligament
region: pelvis-perineum
subregion: Female reproductive organs
taName: Ligamentum latum uteri
summary: Double fold of peritoneum draped over the uterus, tubes and ovaries to the pelvic walls. It carries their vessels, and the ureter runs beneath its base, where it is at risk during hysterectomy.
tags: [female, peritoneum, surgery]
highYield: false
status: draft
anatomy:
  location: >-
    Extends from each side of the [[uterus]] to the lateral pelvic wall and floor, dividing the female pelvic cavity
    into the anterior vesicouterine pouch and the posterior [[rectouterine-pouch]].
  attachments: >-
    Medially: the lateral margin of the uterus, where its two layers become the perimetrium. Laterally and inferiorly:
    the peritoneum of the pelvic wall and floor. Its upper free border encloses the [[uterine-tubes|uterine tube]];
    laterally, above the tube's end, it continues as the suspensory ligament of the [[ovary]].
  function: >-
    A **mesentery** for the uterus, tubes and ovaries (carrying their vessels, nerves and lymphatics); it provides
    little mechanical support.
  relations: >-
    Parts: **mesometrium** (the largest part, beside the uterus), **mesosalpinx** (around the tube) and **mesovarium**
    (attaching the ovary). Between its layers: the uterine tube, the **round ligament of the uterus** and the
    **ligament of the ovary**, the [[uterine-artery]] and uterine venous plexus at its base, the ovarian vessels
    (laterally, in the suspensory ligament), nerves, lymphatics, and mesonephric remnants (epoophoron, paroophoron). The
    [[ureter]] runs forward under the base, **beneath the uterine artery**, about 1–2 cm lateral to the cervix.
  notes: >-
    The dense connective tissue at the base of the broad ligament around the uterine vessels forms the **cardinal
    (transverse cervical) ligaments**, a major uterine support.
clinical:
  - title: Ureteral injury during hysterectomy
    highYield: true
    mechanism: >-
      Clamping the uterine vessels at the base of the broad ligament, or the ovarian vessels in the suspensory ligament
      near the pelvic brim, can include the ureter.
    presentation: Postoperative flank pain, fever, urinoma, hydronephrosis or a ureterovaginal fistula (continuous vaginal leakage of urine).
  - title: Broad ligament hematoma
    highYield: false
    mechanism: Bleeding from uterine vessels after delivery (e.g. a cervical or lower-segment laceration extending laterally) or surgery.
    presentation: Pelvic pain, a paravaginal/adnexal mass and signs of hypovolemia despite little external bleeding.
  - title: Round ligament pain
    highYield: false
    mechanism: Stretching of the round ligament (within the broad ligament) as the uterus grows in the second trimester.
    presentation: Sharp, brief groin or lower-quadrant pain with sudden movement; benign.
pance:

imaging:
  ultrasound: Not seen as a separate structure unless outlined by fluid; paraovarian (paratubal) cysts and broad ligament fibroids appear separate from the ovary.
  mri: Shows broad-ligament fibroids, hematomas and the course of the ureters and vessels.
exam:
  landmarks: Not directly palpable. A mass lateral to the uterus may lie within the broad ligament (fibroid, hematoma, paraovarian cyst).
